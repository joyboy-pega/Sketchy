# 5 Alive — Official Rules

A fast-paced twist on the classic game of "21." Stay under 21, dodge the wild cards, and be the last player with an ALIVE card still standing.

- **Players:** 2–6 (team variant: 4–6)
- **Ages:** 8+
- **Goal:** Be the last player who still has at least one "A-L-I-V-E" card face-up.

---

## 1. Contents

- **72 Number Cards**
  - 1× 7
  - 2× 6
  - 4× 5
  - 8× 4
  - 8× 3
  - 8× 2
  - 8× 1
  - 8× 0
- **Wild Cards**
  - Bomb
  - Hand In & Redeal
  - =0
  - =10
  - Draw 1
  - Draw 2
  - Reverse
  - Skip
  - =21
  - Pass Me By
- **A-L-I-V-E Cards**: each player gets one set of 5 cards spelling A-L-I-V-E.

*(Exact wild card counts vary slightly by edition — check your box insert. The general list above covers the common set.)*

---

## 2. Setup

1. Each player takes one full set of 5 "A-L-I-V-E" cards and places them face-up in front of themselves. These are your lives.
2. Shuffle the number cards and wild cards together into one deck.
3. Deal each player a hand of cards (typically 7 — adjust based on player count and box variant).
4. Place the remaining deck face-down as the draw pile. Flip the top card to start a discard pile if your edition uses one, or simply begin play with a running total of 0.

---

## 3. Objective

Keep the **running total at or under 21**. Get rid of the cards in your hand as fast as possible, and force your opponents to bust (go over 21) — because busting costs them a life.

---

## 4. How to Play

1. Play proceeds clockwise, one player at a time.
2. On your turn, play one card from your hand face-up onto the running total pile.
   - **Number cards** simply add their face value to the running total.
   - **Wild cards** trigger a special effect instead of (or in addition to) adding to the total (see Section 5).
3. If your card would push the running total **over 21**, you **bust**:
   - Flip one of your face-up A-L-I-V-E cards face-down. That's one life lost.
   - The running total resets to 0, and play continues with the next player.
4. If you can't play any card without busting, you may still be forced to play (house rule dependent) or the round resets — follow the specific instructions on any wild card in play.
5. Play continues around the table, running total climbing hand by hand, until someone runs out of cards or is eliminated.

---

## 5. Wild Cards

| Wild Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Bomb** | Forces the target player (or all other players, per instructions on card) to flip an A-L-I-V-E card unless they have a matching escape card (commonly a "0"). |
| **Hand In & Redeal** | All players hand in their current cards and are dealt a fresh hand. |
| **=0** | Resets the running total to 0. |
| **=10** | Sets the running total to exactly 10. |
| **=21** | Sets the running total to exactly 21 — the next player must play very carefully! |
| **Draw 1** | Target player draws 1 extra card from the deck. |
| **Draw 2** | Target player draws 2 extra cards from the deck. |
| **Reverse** | Reverses the direction of play. |
| **Skip** | Skips the next player's turn. |
| **Pass Me By** | Play passes over you — effectively an extra turn skip in your favor. |

*Read the text on each physical wild card carefully, since exact wording/targets can vary by print edition — the table above reflects the commonly published version of the rules.*

---

## 6. Losing Lives & Elimination

- Busting (going over 21) costs you one A-L-I-V-E card (flipped face-down).
- Certain wild cards (like **Bomb**) can also cost you a life directly.
- Once all 5 of your A-L-I-V-E cards are flipped face-down, you are **out of the game**.
- Play continues with remaining players until one player is left — that player wins.

---

## 7. Winning the Game

- **Standard win:** Last player with at least one A-L-I-V-E card still face-up wins.
- **Alternate ending (if the deck runs out or by house rule):** As soon as one player flips all 5 A-L-I-V-E cards, the game can instead end immediately, and the player with the **most** A-L-I-V-E cards remaining wins.
  - **Tiebreaker:** Tied players add up the face value of the number cards remaining in their hand. Lowest total wins.

---

## 8. Sudden Death Variant

For a faster game:

- Each player starts with only **1 A-L-I-V-E card** instead of 5.
- One bust or one Bomb hit and you're eliminated immediately.
- Great for quick replays or breaking a tie between two finalists.

---

## 9. Team Play Variant (4–6 Players)

1. Divide players into 2 teams, seated so teammates alternate around the table (not sitting next to each other).
2. Each **team** shares a single set of A-L-I-V-E cards (one life pool per team, not per player).
3. Wild cards function as normal, but effects that cost a life apply to the **team's** shared pool.
4. When any player empties their hand of cards completely, the **opposing team** loses only **one** life (not one per player).
5. Play continues normally until one team flips all of its A-L-I-V-E cards. The surviving team wins.

---

## 10. Quick Reference

- Running total must stay ≤ 21.
- Bust → flip 1 A-L-I-V-E card, total resets to 0.
- 5 flipped A-L-I-V-E cards = eliminated.
- Last player (or team) standing wins.
